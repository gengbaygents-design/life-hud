PHASE GOAL
==================================================

"HOME tells you what matters now.

Source domains remain authoritative.

==================================================
ATTENTION ENGINE V1
==================================================

Implement deterministic rule-based Attention Engine.

NO LLM required.

NO ML.

NO fake numeric priority shown to user.

Internal scoring/ranking may exist,
but user-facing explanation must be natural.

Consider factors such as:

- hard deadline urgency
- current real-world event
- planned-now relevance
- importance
- estimated duration fit
- status / energy fit
- task dependency availability
- interruption cost
- explicit user intent
- current time of day

Keep rules understandable and testable.
